Our Story

Sweet Crumbs began with a simple idea: create pastries that bring people together. What started as a small home kitchen has grown into a bakery dedicated to beautiful, delicious creations.

Every cake, pastry and confection is carefully prepared using quality ingredients and traditional baking techniques.

From everyday treats to special celebrations, we believe that every occasion deserves something sweet.
